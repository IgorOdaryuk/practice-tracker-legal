Privacy Policy for Practice Journal

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Practice Journal (listed in the App Store as Practice Journal for Musicians) is a practice timer for musicians. It is made and published by Igor Odaryuk, an independent developer, who is responsible for this app and this policy. The app was built to work entirely on your device, and this policy describes what that means in practice.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or any other personal data, and we do not sell or share any data, because we never receive it.

Where your practice data lives

Practice sessions, exercises, notes, your daily goal, your streak and your roadmap answers are written to a database file inside the app's own storage on your device. That data is never uploaded to us. We operate no server that receives it, and we cannot read it.

If your device backs itself up (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Google's backup on Android), the app's data may be included in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies, not by us. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Backup files you make yourself

The Backup screen can save a file with your sessions, your own exercises and your settings. The app hands that file to your phone's share sheet, and it goes only where you send it, such as Files, a cloud drive or an email to yourself. The app does not send it anywhere on its own. Your purchase is never part of that file.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your device. They are created and shown by the phone itself and do not pass through any server.

Purchases

The app offers a free trial period and a one-time purchase that unlocks the progress screens. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, card details or billing address. The app only ever learns whether a purchase exists on your store account, so it knows what to unlock.

Network connections

The app contacts the App Store or Google Play to check purchases and prices, and opens this policy and the support page in a browser when you tap them. No other network requests are made.

If you email us

When you write to the support address, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to answer you and to fix what you reported, we do not add it to any mailing list, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed for that. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Because your practice data stays on your device, you control it directly: you can see, edit, export and delete every session in the app, and deleting the app removes all of it. For anything you have sent us by email, you can ask to see it, correct it or have it deleted by writing to the address below, and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
