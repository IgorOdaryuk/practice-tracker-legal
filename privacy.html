Privacy Policy — Practice Tracker

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Practice Tracker is a practice timer for musicians. It was built to work entirely on your device. This policy describes what that means in practice.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or any other personal data.

Where your practice data lives

Practice sessions, exercises, notes, your daily goal, your streak and your roadmap answers are written to a database file inside the app's own storage on your device. That data is never uploaded to us. We operate no server that receives it, and we cannot read it.

If your device backs itself up (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Google's backup on Android), the app's data may be included in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies, not by us. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Purchases

The app offers a free trial period and a one-time purchase that unlocks the progress screens. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details or billing address. The app only ever learns whether a purchase exists on your store account, so it knows what to unlock.

Network connections

  • The app contacts the App Store or Google Play to check purchases and prices.
  • Lesson links open in your browser or in the YouTube app. Once a link opens, that service's own privacy policy applies to what happens there.

No other network requests are made.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
