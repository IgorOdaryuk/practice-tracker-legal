Reporting a bug

Four lines make the difference between a fix today and a week of questions back and forth:

  • What you did, step by step, right before it went wrong.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Your device and system version (Settings → General → About).
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of the App Store page.

A screenshot or a short screen recording is worth all four.

Common questions

Where is my practice data kept? Does it sync?

On your device, in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded, so there is no sync between devices and no copy on a server. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) usually includes it, which covers a new phone set up from the old one — but not a phone set up as new, not deleting and reinstalling the app, and not moving between iPhone and Android.

How do I keep a copy of my practice?

Report tab → Backup → Save a copy. That writes one file with every session and your settings, and hands it to your phone's share sheet: put it in Files, a cloud drive, or mail it to yourself. Nothing is sent anywhere except where you choose.

On the new phone, install the app and use Restore from a file on the same screen. It adds back what is missing and leaves anything already there alone, so restoring twice by mistake changes nothing. It works between iPhone and Android in either direction.

I paid, then changed phones. How do I get it back?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same store account you bought with. Nothing is charged twice. Your purchase is not part of the backup file — the store keeps it, which is why it comes back even if you never made a backup.

How long is the free period, and what happens after?

Seven days with everything open. After that the timer and Beats stay free and keep recording your sessions — the weekly report, the month calendar, the streak, the full history and your roadmap need the one-time unlock. Nothing you logged is deleted; it waits for you.

Does the timer keep running when I lock my phone?

Yes. Elapsed time is measured from the moment you pressed start, so locking the screen, switching apps, or the app being closed by the system cannot lose those minutes. If the app is killed mid-session, it offers the session back when you return.

I forgot to press start. Can I add it afterwards?

Yes — Report → All sessions → +. Pick the exercise, the length and when it happened. You can edit or delete anything already logged from the same screen.

How do I change my daily goal?

Tap the gear on the streak card at the top of the Report tab.

Can I delete everything and start over?

Delete individual sessions from the history screen, or remove the app to erase all of it at once.
